## OctoAcme Project Management Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured five-phase project lifecycle to deliver customer value consistently while maintaining team alignment, managing risk proactively, and continuously evolving processes based on experience and team feedback.

**Project Lifecycle & Workflows**
The organization follows a comprehensive five-phase lifecycle: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective. During initiation, business need is validated through a lightweight one-pager capturing problem statements, success metrics, and stakeholder alignment. Planning breaks work into shippable increments using a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria and story point estimation. Execution is managed iteratively through sprint-based delivery with small pull requests (≤400 lines), continuous integration testing, and a project board with columns for Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done. Each phase includes defined deliverables and checklists to ensure consistent, repeatable execution.

**Defined Roles & Responsibilities**
OctoAcme recognizes distinct personas with clear ownership: Project Managers coordinate delivery, manage schedules, risks, and stakeholder communication; Product Managers define what should be built, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes through data-driven decisions; Developers implement features, write tests, and participate in design and code reviews; and QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance criteria. Each role has specific communication touchpoints and responsibilities, creating clear accountability and reducing ambiguity about decision ownership and deliverable accountability.

**Communication Cadence & Risk Management**
The organization maintains consistent communication through daily standups (15 minutes), weekly syncs between PM and Product Lead, twice-weekly team standups, and monthly stakeholder updates. Risks are actively managed through a Risk Register tracking ID, Description, Impact, Probability, Owner, and Mitigation plans. The escalation framework is three-tiered: team-level triage in daily standups, PM escalation to Product Leads and dependent teams, and sponsor-level escalation for business-impacting issues. This structured approach ensures visibility, transparency, and rapid resolution of blockers.

**Quality Assurance & Continuous Improvement**
Quality is embedded throughout delivery with unit tests for new logic, integration tests where applicable, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning in CI, and manual QA for feature acceptance. After each sprint, release, or milestone, the team conducts retrospectives to capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements tracked through the backlog. The culture emphasizes psychological safety, data-informed decisions, iterative delivery, and continuous learning, enabling OctoAcme to improve processes systematically based on real execution experience.

## Key OctoAcme Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named PM and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning
